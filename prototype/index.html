Zijin CAI 蔡子晋

PhD in Artificial Intelligence, CUHK-Shenzhen · Research Fellow, CUHK(SZ)–CROSS Joint Laboratory

I work on multimodal perception and generative models: teaching models to see what happens over time, and making diffusion fine-tuning cheap enough to iterate on. Most of what I build turns into research infrastructure — pipelines, agent tooling and evaluation harnesses that make the research repeatable. Before this, an MSc in Data Science at CUHK-Shenzhen and a BSc in Economics at Manchester.

Now — shipping omarchy · dsh, building AI research infrastructure, and multimodal agents that have to hold state.
